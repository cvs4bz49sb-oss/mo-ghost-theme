The Greek Fathers

Patrologia Graeca · Clement of Rome to the fall of Constantinople

Migne’s Greek Fathers run from the Apostolic Fathers through the doctors of Alexandria, Cappadocia and Antioch to the Byzantine writers who followed them until the fifteenth century. Every text is in Greek with the Latin translation Migne printed beside it. The library adds English.

1 · The edition

A hundred and sixty-one volumes

Between 1857 and 1866 Jacques-Paul Migne printed the Greek Fathers at his workshop outside Paris. The Greek stands in one column and a Latin translation in the other.

2 · Where they wrote

From Alexandria to the City

The Greek Fathers wrote in the great sees of the East: Alexandria, Antioch, Jerusalem, Caesarea and the cities of Cappadocia. From the fourth century they also wrote in Constantinople. The city drew writers to itself for a thousand years. Each circle is a place where a writer lived or served.
