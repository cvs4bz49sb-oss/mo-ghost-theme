1 · Where they taught

From Wittenberg

Lutheranism was made in the universities. Luther and Melanchthon taught at Wittenberg. Their students carried the Reformation to Jena, Königsberg, Helmstedt, Rostock and Copenhagen. For two hundred years the orthodox theologians moved between these universities.
