The Councils

Collected by Giovanni Domenico Mansi · Sacrorum Conciliorum Nova et Amplissima Collectio

Mansi’s collection is a shelf of its own. It holds the councils of the early Church and then those of the Roman Catholic Church. It runs from the apostles at Jerusalem to the First Vatican Council. Eastern synods continue to 1902. Their canons, letters, sessions and signatures are printed in Greek and Latin page by page. English stands beside them.

    Chapter one

    A shelf that took 170 years

    The first thirty-one tomes appeared from 1757. Mansi began them. Editors at Florence and Venice finished them after his death. They carry the councils in order of time to the Council of Florence. Between 1901 and 1927 Louis Petit and Jean-Baptiste Martin reprinted them with supplements for the publisher H. Welter. They continued the record to 1870.
