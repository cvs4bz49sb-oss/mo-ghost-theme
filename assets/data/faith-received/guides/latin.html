The Latin Fathers

Patrologia Latina · Tertullian to Innocent III

Migne’s Latin Fathers hold a thousand years of the Latin church in one row of books. They run from the African apologists and the age of Augustine through the monks and missionaries of the early Middle Ages to Bernard and the masters of the twelfth century. Every page is in Latin with English beside it.

1 · The edition

Two hundred and twenty-one volumes

Migne’s workshop at Montrouge near Paris printed the Latin writers in 217 volumes between 1844 and 1855. Four volumes of indexes followed in 1862–1864.

2 · Where they wrote

From Carthage to Clairvaux

Latin Christian writing begins in Africa and settles in Italy. Then it moves north with the monks to Spain and Gaul, to Ireland and Northumbria, and to the Rhine and the abbeys of Burgundy. Each circle is a place where a writer lived or served.

    3 · How to read it

    From a column to a page

    Chapters two to four of Anselm’s Proslogion stand in volume 158 at columns 227 and 228. The page here is set in type from that text. The columns and the letters are Migne’s.

      4 · Find

      Authors and volumes

      A reference such as PL 158:227C opens volume 158 at column 227, letter C. Odd columns stand on the left of the page and even columns on the right.
